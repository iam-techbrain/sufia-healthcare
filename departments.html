Institutional Overview & Objectives

Commitment to accessible, timely, coordinated, and ethical healthcare.

Sufia Healthcare Pvt. Ltd. provides comprehensive healthcare services through an integrated multidisciplinary clinical and diagnostic facility. The organization is dedicated to delivering accessible, timely, coordinated, and quality healthcare across outpatient, emergency, inpatient, surgical, critical-care, paediatric, gynecological, urological, laboratory, and radiological services.

Core Organizational Objective

To provide comprehensive, compassionate, and standardized healthcare under one roof covering the entire care continuum: OPD ➔ Diagnostics ➔ Emergency ➔ Inpatient Care ➔ Surgery ➔ ICU/NICU/PICU ➔ Discharge & Rehabilitation.

Critical Care: ICU, NICU & PICU Scope

Multi-disciplinary intensive care units with invasive life-support systems.

ICU (Adult Intensive Care Unit)

Equipped with modern multipara monitors, ventilators, central oxygen, defibrillators, infusion pumps, and round-the-clock intensivists for post-op, cardiac, respiratory, and sepsis cases.

NICU (Neonatal Intensive Care Unit)

Specialized care for preterm, low-birth-weight, and jaundiced newborns with open incubators, phototherapy units, neonatal CPAP, and warmers.

PICU (Paediatric Intensive Care Unit)

Intensive hemodynamic monitoring and advanced pediatric resuscitation for acute paediatric medical and post-surgical crises.
