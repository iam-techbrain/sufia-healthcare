OUR MISSION & HERITAGE

Bringing Super Speciality Care Closer to Home

Sufia Super Speciality Hospital was founded with a clear, singular vision: to bridge the critical healthcare gap in North Bihar. For decades, patients and families in Siwan and surrounding districts had to travel hundreds of kilometers to Patna, Gorakhpur, or Varanasi for emergency surgeries, neonatal intensive care, or advanced diagnostics.

Today, Sufia Healthcare stands as a premier multispecialty hospital offering state-of-the-art infrastructure: three modular operation theatres with HEPA filtration, dedicated adult ICU, Neonatal ICU (NICU), Paediatric ICU (PICU), round-the-clock CT scan, automated pathology laboratory, and 24x7 emergency and trauma services.
