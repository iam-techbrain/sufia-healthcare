24/7 CRITICAL CARE UNITS

2. Intensive Care & Emergency Facilities

🚨

24×7 Trauma Unit

Rapid clinical triage, acute trauma resuscitation, minor emergency procedures, and instant OT/ICU transfer coordination.

🛏️

Intensive Care Unit (ICU)

Continuous monitoring of critically ill patients, post-op critical care, respiratory & hemodynamic monitoring & specialist coordination.

👶

Neonatal ICU (NICU)

Dedicated care for premature & low-birth-weight newborns, respiratory support, infection management, and newborn stabilization.

👧

Paediatric ICU (PICU)

Continuous monitoring of critically ill paediatric patients, severe infection management, and post-operative paediatric critical care.

DIAGNOSTICS & INFRASTRUCTURE

3. 24×7 Diagnostics, OTs & Pharmacy

🩻

24×7 Radiology & CT Scan

Round-the-clock CT Scan facility, Digital X-ray examinations, Ultrasonography (USG), and emergency diagnostic imaging with qualified radiologist reporting.

🧪

In-House Pathology Lab

Comprehensive diagnostic support including Hematology, Biochemistry, Clinical Pathology, Serology, routine blood & urine tests, and instant ICU monitoring labs.

🏥

3 Modular Operation Theatres

Three state-of-the-art modular operation theatres (OTs) equipped with HEPA laminar airflow for elective & emergency surgeries across all specialties.

💊

24×7 In-House Pharmacy

24×7 pharmacy facility dispensing prescribed medicines and healthcare products for both OPD patients and hospitalized IPD patients.

🍱

In-House Patient Dietary

Personalized in-house dietary & nutrition service for IPD patients, delivering hygienic meals in accordance with doctor-prescribed diets.

💳

Insurance & TPA Helpdesk

Pre-authorisation filing, document submission, query resolution, and cashless claim processing with major insurance & TPA partners.
