Overview

Comprehensive multidisciplinary healthcare services

Sufia Healthcare Pvt. Ltd. provides comprehensive healthcare services through a multidisciplinary clinical and diagnostic setup. The organization is committed to delivering accessible, timely, coordinated, and quality healthcare services to patients across outpatient, emergency, inpatient, surgical, critical-care, pediatric, gynecological, urological, laboratory, and radiological services.

Round-the-Clock Support Services

Key round-the-clock support services include Emergency, Diagnostic Radiology, and In-House Pharmacy services for both OPD and IPD patients.

02

Clinical & Medical Services

Multidisciplinary medical and clinical care

2.1 Obstetrics & Gynaecology

  • Outpatient consultation and evaluation.
  • Antenatal and postnatal care.
  • Management of routine and high-risk pregnancies.
  • Obstetric emergencies.
  • Normal and assisted deliveries, as clinically indicated.
  • Caesarean section and other obstetric procedures.
  • Management of gynaecological conditions.
  • Menstrual and hormonal disorder management.
  • Infertility evaluation and related consultation.
  • Preventive women's healthcare and counselling.
  • Gynaecological surgical procedures, as clinically indicated.
  • Post-operative and post-delivery follow-up care.

2.2 General Surgery

  • General surgical consultations.
  • Pre-operative assessment and preparation.
  • Emergency surgical evaluation.
  • Elective and emergency surgical procedures.
  • Abdominal and gastrointestinal surgical conditions.
  • Hernia and related surgical procedures.
  • Appendicitis and other acute surgical conditions.
  • Gallbladder and biliary tract conditions.
  • Soft-tissue and minor surgical procedures.
  • Wound management.
  • Post-operative monitoring and care.
  • Surgical follow-up and rehabilitation support.

2.3 Paediatric Care

  • Paediatric OPD consultations.
  • Evaluation and treatment of common childhood illnesses.
  • Management of infections and acute illnesses.
  • Growth and developmental assessment.
  • Nutritional assessment and counselling.
  • Newborn and infant care.
  • Paediatric emergency care.
  • Immunization-related consultation and guidance.
  • Management and monitoring of paediatric patients requiring hospitalization.
  • Referral and specialist coordination where required.
03

Emergency & Critical Care Services

Round-the-clock emergency and intensive care

3.1 Emergency Department

  • 24×7 emergency medical services with round-the-clock assessment, stabilization, treatment, and coordination for medical, surgical, paediatric, and obstetric emergencies.
  • Initial emergency assessment and triage.
  • Stabilization of critically ill or injured patients.
  • Management of medical and surgical emergencies.
  • Emergency paediatric care.
  • Emergency obstetric and gynaecological care.
  • Emergency investigations and diagnostics.
  • Coordination with ICU, NICU, PICU, OT, and other departments as clinically required.
  • Referral and transfer coordination for cases requiring higher-level care.

3.2 Intensive Care Unit (ICU)

  • Continuous monitoring of critically ill patients.
  • Management of patients requiring intensive medical and nursing care.
  • Critical-care support following major surgery or acute illness.
  • Management of medical emergencies requiring intensive monitoring.
  • Respiratory and hemodynamic monitoring and support.
  • Coordination with specialist physicians and surgeons.
  • Critical-care nursing and supportive services.
  • Laboratory and radiology coordination for ICU patients.

3.3 Neonatal Intensive Care Unit (NICU)

  • Care of premature and low-birth-weight newborns.
  • Monitoring and management of sick neonates.
  • Respiratory support as clinically indicated.
  • Management of neonatal infections and other neonatal conditions.
  • Newborn stabilization.
  • Monitoring of vital parameters.
  • Nutritional and supportive neonatal care.
  • Coordination with Obstetrics & Gynaecology and Paediatric departments.
  • Referral/transfer coordination for cases requiring advanced neonatal care.

3.4 Paediatric Intensive Care Unit (PICU)

  • Continuous monitoring of critically ill paediatric patients.
  • Management of severe infections and acute illnesses.
  • Respiratory and supportive care.
  • Post-operative paediatric critical care.
  • Emergency stabilization.
  • Coordination with Paediatric, General Surgery, Emergency, Laboratory, and Radiology departments.
  • Referral and transfer coordination when advanced care is required.

  • Evaluation of urinary infections and related disorders.

Laboratory Services

In-house diagnostic support

The in-house laboratory provides diagnostic support for OPD, emergency, inpatient, surgical, ICU, NICU, and PICU services.

  • Hematology investigations.
  • Biochemistry investigations.
  • Clinical pathology.
  • Serology and related investigations.
  • Routine blood and urine investigations.
  • Pre-operative investigations.
  • Emergency laboratory investigations.
  • Monitoring investigations for ICU, NICU, and PICU patients.
  • Other investigations as available within the laboratory's scope and applicable approvals.
07

Radiology & Diagnostic Imaging

Diagnostic imaging across hospital departments

  • CT Scan facility available to support emergency, inpatient, ICU, NICU, PICU, OPD, surgical, gynaecological, paediatric, and urological diagnostic requirements.
  • 24×7 CT Scan availability for urgent and emergency diagnostic requirements, subject to clinical indication and operational availability.
  • 24×7 diagnostic radiology services to support emergency, inpatient, ICU, NICU, PICU, OPD, surgical, gynaecology, paediatric, and urology patients.
  • X-ray examinations.
  • Ultrasonography (USG).
  • Other imaging services available at the facility.
  • Emergency diagnostic imaging.
  • Pre-operative investigations.
  • Imaging support for OPD and admitted patients.
  • Imaging support for ICU, NICU, PICU, Emergency, Surgery, Gynaecology, Paediatrics, and Urology departments.
  • Reporting and interpretation by appropriately qualified professionals.

Hospital Infrastructure & Support Facilities

Dedicated hospital infrastructure

  • In-house food and dietary services for IPD patients, including meals according to prescribed dietary requirements and hospital protocols.
  • Three modular operation theatres (OTs) to support elective and emergency surgical procedures.
  • Dedicated PICU facilities for paediatric patients requiring intensive care.
  • Dedicated NICU facilities for neonatal patients requiring specialized intensive care.
  • Dedicated ICU facilities for patients requiring intensive and critical care.
  • Dedicated wards and inpatient facilities for the respective clinical services.

Scope Limitation

Availability and applicable clinical requirements

The availability of any particular procedure, investigation, treatment, specialist, equipment, or facility shall be subject to the hospital's infrastructure, staffing, applicable licences/registrations, clinical requirements, and availability at the relevant time.

Medical diagnosis, treatment decisions, surgical procedures, prescriptions, and clinical management shall be undertaken by appropriately qualified and authorized healthcare professionals.
