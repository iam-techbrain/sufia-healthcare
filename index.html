About Sufia Super Speciality Hospital

High-quality, accessible & reliable healthcare services

Sufia Super Speciality Hospital is dedicated to delivering high-quality, accessible, and reliable medical services. Equipped with advanced infrastructure and state-of-the-art medical facilities, the focus is on accurate diagnosis and effective treatment across a wide range of health conditions.

With a patient-first approach, efficiency, and attention, every effort is made to ensure a safe, comfortable, and well-managed healthcare experience for patients and their families.

We are committed to maintaining high standards in treatment and patient care, the aim is to provide the best healthcare services that people can trust.

OFFICIAL CLINICAL SCOPE & CARE PATHWAY

Comprehensive Scope of Healthcare Services

Full breakdown of inpatient, outpatient, emergency, surgical, ICU, diagnostic, and cashless insurance services as certified by Sufia Healthcare Pvt. Ltd.

Integrated Care Pathway: OPD ➔ Diagnostics ➔ Emergency ➔ Inpatient Care ➔ Surgery ➔ ICU/NICU/PICU ➔ Discharge & Follow-up
📄 Download Scope of Services (PDF) 📝 View Scope of Services (TXT)
🩺

1. Clinical & OPD Services

  • ✓ Obstetrics & Gynaecology: Antenatal/postnatal care, high-risk pregnancy, normal & C-section deliveries, gynaec surgeries.
  • ✓ General Surgery: Emergency & elective procedures, abdominal/GI, hernia, appendicitis, gallbladder & wound care.
  • ✓ Paediatric Care: General OPD, infections, growth & developmental monitoring, vaccination guidance.
  • ✓ Urology Services: Kidney & urinary stones, UTI management, prostate care, urological surgical procedures.
🚑

2. Emergency & Critical Care

  • ✓ 24×7 Trauma Unit: Triage assessment, acute trauma resuscitation, interdepartmental OT & ICU transfer.
  • ✓ ICU (Intensive Care Unit): Continuous hemodynamic & respiratory support for critically ill patients.
  • ✓ NICU (Neonatal ICU): Premature & low-birth-weight newborn stabilization & intensive monitoring.
  • ✓ PICU (Paediatric ICU): Specialized paediatric critical care & post-operative recovery.
🏥

3. Surgical & OT Facilities

  • ✓ 3 Modular Operation Theatres (OTs): Equipped for elective & emergency surgeries with HEPA filtration.
  • ✓ Pre & Post-Op Care: Comprehensive surgical evaluation, anaesthesia support, recovery suites.
  • ✓ Specialized IPD Wards: Dedicated inpatient hospitalization units & continuous nursing care.
  • ✓ In-House Dietary Services: Customized clinical nutrition & patient meal plans according to medical protocols.
🩻

4. Radiology & Diagnostic Imaging

  • ✓ 24×7 CT Scan Facility: Round-the-clock CT imaging for emergency, trauma, ICU, OPD & surgical patients.
  • ✓ 24×7 Diagnostic Radiology: Digital X-ray examinations & Ultrasonography (USG).
  • ✓ Pre-Op & Emergency Imaging: Immediate radiologist reporting & diagnostic evaluation.
🧪

5. Laboratory & Pharmacy

  • ✓ In-House Pathology Lab: Hematology, Biochemistry, Clinical Pathology & Serology.
  • ✓ Emergency Lab Testing: Instant blood & urine investigations for ICU, NICU, PICU & Emergency.
  • ✓ 24×7 In-House Pharmacy: Round-the-clock prescribed medicine dispensing for OPD & IPD.
🛡️

6. Cashless TPA & Quality Standards

  • ✓ Insurance & TPA Cashless Helpdesk: Pre-authorization filing, query resolution & claim processing.
  • ✓ Quality & Safety Protocols: NABH safety guidelines, infection control & strict patient data privacy.
  • ✓ Discharge & Follow-Up Care: Structured post-discharge documentation & specialist follow-up planning.
